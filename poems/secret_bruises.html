Secret Bruises

25 Feb, 2024

You disappeared, north.
Left with noble intentions,
but what about me?

A suit of armour
too big for a little boy
against beasts, monsters.

The fruit and serpent,
a lost and hungry ghost, I
sought warmth from the womb.

I sat with myself,
the secret bruises laid bare.
Terrified, I'd run.

Safe in my fortress;
alone and under the bed,
talking to myself.
