The Colour of Blueberries

02 Sep, 2024

She tasted the weight of their tender love
And it bruised the native scarlet of her heart
Into the colour of blueberries
So she tried to climb mountains more than her might
With her little hands and her little feet
Enough to be in their eyes, their ears, their mouths
Just to inherit the injury
Of forgetting how to be herself
