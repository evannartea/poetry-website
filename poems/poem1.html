A Familiar Love

11 Mar, 2026

She threw warmth around my shoulders
Spilling honey at the seams
I'd flinch and thrash
At the softest touch
Feigning loveless abandon
A vision of grief upon a closer look

My nakedness caught beneath the searchlight
Left me scrambling for fig leaves
And still she'd say it's okay, my dear
With an unwavering hand at every retreat

Suddenly, I'd look much younger
But the gentle words she spoke
Were foreign to me
Not those of a familiar love—
A kind guided by absence
