Saṃsāra

13 Nov, 2024

The little ghost runs away from himself
It's too loud here, I say to him
And you can't seem to remember who you were
Your hardened skin and injured words
Born from your mother's pain
In a foreign land that you call home

I cry for the anguished creature
Who rubs his chest to soothe his broken heart and broken soul
He surrenders himself to hands in higher places
Teaching him that to love is to sacrifice oneself
Though you still have time to find your own truths
To err despite your piety, to die the ephemeral death

Innocence is returning to the wanderer's reflection
Not learning but recollecting
Echoes from a previous existence, before this earthly realm
Singing blissfully, you're coming back to yourself, to me!
Slowly, dying slowly
To finally reach our eternal soul
