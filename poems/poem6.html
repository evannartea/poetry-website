Tin Man

21 Aug, 2024

I thundered and bellowed from behind the curtain
And you were fooled by the might of witches and lions
Blinded by my own weakness, I tried to wear some courage
Using a cape of grass and a terracotta crown
Then truth became blurred
Well, I am just a rusted tin man, longing for my heart
Strip me of illusion, undo my truths
You will see something hollow
